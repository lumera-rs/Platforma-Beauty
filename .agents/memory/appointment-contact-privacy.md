---
name: Appointment contact privacy
description: Rules for releasing a salon's exact location and contact details to customers.
---

Public discovery and salon profiles must never contain a salon's street address, phone, email, or coordinates. Exact details are available only to the customer who owns a qualifying appointment, and only while its status is `pending`, `confirmed`, or `completed`.

**Why:** City and municipality support discovery while preventing unauthenticated visitors—and non-qualifying customers—from bypassing marketplace booking and privacy safeguards.

**How to apply:** Any new public salon response, card, cache, or client view must use the redacted public contract. Any protected contact path must scope by customer ownership and use an explicit qualifying-status allowlist rather than blocking only cancelled appointments.
