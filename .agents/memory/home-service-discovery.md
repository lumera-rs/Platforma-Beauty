---
name: Home-service discovery
description: Keeps public home-service discovery aligned with the services customers can actually book at their address.
---

Public discovery must calculate and filter home-service availability from active services marked as available for home visits.

**Why:** A salon-level flag can become stale or disagree with its individual services, causing a mobile-service filter to return cards that cannot accept the requested kind of booking.

**How to apply:** When changing service availability or any public list, card, shelf, or filter that advertises home visits, use the same active-service rule as appointment creation.
